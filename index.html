Koen van den Bosch

PhD Candidate in Finance  ·  Rotterdam School of Management

I am a PhD candidate in Finance at the Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University. My research focuses on insurance markets, climate and disaster risk, housing, and financial intermediation, with an emphasis on optimal macroprudential policy.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market. My job market paper, A Theory of Optimal Disaster Insurance under Intermediary Constraints, studies how insurer financing constraints shape the availability of insurance in weather-exposed housing markets.

Short Bio

I joined the PhD program in Finance at RSM, Erasmus University in September 2022 and expect to graduate in 2027. During the PhD, I was a PhD intern at the International Monetary Fund (Summer 2025), working on insurance markets in the Americas, and a visiting PhD student at the Swedish House of Finance at the Stockholm School of Economics, hosted by Marcus Opp. I am part of the Erasmus Initiative Dynamics of Inclusive Prosperity.

Before the PhD, I was an Associate in the Strategy and Technology Group at Aon (2021–2022), advising global insurance companies using data and analytics, and a consultant for emerging markets at Larive International (2019–2021), working on business development in Sub-Saharan Africa and South-East Asia.

I hold an MSc in Economics (cum laude, top 5% of the class) and a BSc in Economics and Business Economics from Tilburg University, with an exchange at Universidad del Pacífico in Peru. My MSc thesis on precautionary saving and credit opportunities in Nigeria and Mongolia was a finalist for the Jan Brouwer Thesis Award.

Outside of research, I play the piano, kitesurf, mountain bike, and read. I rowed competitively at Tilburg University with top-sport status and was treasurer of the student rowing association T.S.R. Vidar.
